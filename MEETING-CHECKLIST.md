## THE MEETING (45 Minutes)

### **MIN 0-2: GREETING & RAPPORT**
```
"Great to meet you! 
I know you're busy, so I'll be quick - this is a 15-minute pitch 
followed by a demo of your specific data. Sound good?"
```

### **MIN 2-5: PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION**
Ask them:
- "What's your biggest challenge with direct bookings?"
- "How much are you paying in OTA commissions right now?"
- "Do you know which guest segments are most valuable?"
- "How often do your guests repeat?"

*Listen. Take notes. Use their words in your pitch.*

**Open with:** "Here's the brutal truth - most hotels are leaving $5-10 million on the table every year by not understanding their guests properly."

**Walk through:**

3. The ROI (show the numbers)
4. Timeline (how fast they see results)

**Key stats to mention:**
- "+$10.5M revenue for 500-room hotel"
- "15% more direct bookings"
- "2% email response becomes 4.5%"
- "Payback in 6 days"
- "20,900% ROI"

### **MIN 35-40: ANSWER OBJECTIONS**

**If they say:** "We already use Booking.com"
**You say:** "Booking.com is good for short-term volume. We help you convert 15% more guests directly - that's $1.8M in commissions staying in your pocket instead of going to them."

**If they say:** "This looks complicated"
**You say:** "I get that. We handle all the tech. You just log in, see your metrics, and run campaigns. Your team will spend 30 minutes/week in the system."

**If they say:** "We don't have budget"
**You say:** "Zero risk - we do revenue share. You only pay 2% of the extra bookings we generate. In week 2, you'll see $50K in extra revenue, so you pay us $1K. Pure profit for you."

**The goal:** Get them to say YES to ONE of:
- ✅ Free trial

## WHAT TO SAY WHEN THEY ASK THESE QUESTIONS

### "How does this integrate with our PMS?"
**Answer:** "We connect to Opera, Micros, Skytouch, and most modern PMS systems. We pull booking data automatically, so you don't have to manually enter anything. Our tech team handles the integration - takes about 4 hours."

### "What if we have 100K guests, not 70K?"
**Answer:** "No problem! Our system scales to 1 million+ guests. It actually gets better the more data you have - more patterns, better predictions."

### "Can our team edit guest data?"
**Answer:** "Yes! Multiple team members can log in with different permission levels. Staff can add notes, update preferences, tag VIP guests. It's designed for your whole team."

### "How do you verify emails?"
**Answer:** "We check three things: (1) Is the syntax correct? (2) Does the domain have mail servers? (3) Optionally, we can probe the actual mailbox (this requires a paid verification service). Most hotels want at least the first two."

### "Can we export this data?"
**Answer:** "Absolutely. CSV, Excel, JSON - export anytime. It's your data. We just help you use it better."

### "What if we want to cancel?"
**Answer:** "You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. No penalties. We'll export all your data. Usually by month 2, the ROI is so clear that hotels want to stay, but we never lock anyone in."
