HOW TO GENERATE PDF (Choose One Method)
================================================================================

METHOD 1: BROWSER PRINT-TO-PDF (Recommended)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Open either HTML file in your web browser:
   - Double-click the .html file, OR
   - Right-click → Open with → Chrome/Firefox/Edge

2. Press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to open Print dialog

3. Select:
   - Destination: "Save as PDF"
   - Margins: "Minimal" or "None"
   - Background Graphics: ☑ Enabled
   - Paper size: A4

4. Click "Save" and choose location

5. Done! Your PDF is ready to email
