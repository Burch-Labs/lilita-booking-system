## 💼 3 PRICING MODELS TO PRESENT

### Model 1: Monthly Subscription
**For:** Conservative clients who like predictability
- Starter: $500/month (small hotel)
- Growth: $1,500/month (medium hotel)
- Enterprise: $3,000/month (large chain)
- Best for: Hotels that trust they'll see ROI

### Model 2: Revenue Share
**For:** Performance-driven clients
- Platform: 2% of extra direct bookings
- Minimum: $500/month
- Maximum: $5,000/month
- Best for: Skeptical owners, want proof first

### Model 3: Hybrid (Most Popular)
**For:** Win-win situation
- Base: $800/month
- Success bonus: 1% of incremental revenue above baseline
- Best for: Everyone - aligned incentives

**How to present:** 
"Which model works best for you? Most prefer the hybrid - we both win when you win."
