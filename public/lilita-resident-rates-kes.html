Terms & Conditions

Resident Eligibility

Resident rates apply to East African passport holders (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, DRC) with valid residency documentation. Non-residents will be charged the standard international rates. Proof of residency (national ID, passport, or residency card) required at time of booking.

Child Policy

Minimum age is 6 years. Child rates are applicable for up to two children aged 12 and younger sharing with two adults in a family tent. Should the family tent be unavailable, child rates are applicable for one child aged 12 and younger sharing with one or two adults in a tent. Children over 12 years pay the applicable adult rates and will be accommodated in a separate tent. Child residents receive 20% discount on applicable rates.

Insurance

Clients are strongly advised to have comprehensive travel and medical insurance for their period of stay, giving full cover against any unforeseen incidents.

Bookings & Cancellations

Provisional bookings will be held for a maximum of 14 days. Bookings will be confirmed only upon receipt of a 30% deposit. The balance is payable 45 days prior to arrival.

Cancellation Policy:

  • More than 45 days prior to arrival: Deposit refunded (net of bank charges)
  • 44-31 days prior to arrival: 50% of full value forfeited
  • 30 days or less / No-show: 100% of full value forfeited

Check In / Check Out

Check-in: 12:00 hrs | Check-out: 10:00 hrs

Accommodation

Lilita Keper features 12 luxury tented suites, each with all modern comforts of a 5-star boutique hotel. Suites can be arranged in twin or king configurations. Our reservations team will work to fulfill specific rooming requests, however this cannot be guaranteed.

Park Fees

Park fees are payable to Kenya Wildlife Service upon entry into the Maasai Mara National Reserve. Fees are valid for 24 hours from time of entry. For any additional hours spent in the park, guests must pay for another full 24-hour period. Fees are subject to change as guided by relevant authorities.

Access

By Air: Approximately 45 minutes flight time from Wilson Airport to Lilita Keper airstrip. Flight times and minimum passenger numbers are at the discretion of the flight operator.

By Road: Approximately 4-5 hours drive from Nairobi depending on weather and road conditions.

Rate Changes

Rates are subject to change due to unforeseen circumstances including, but not limited to, currency fluctuations, changes in taxes and levies, government decree, or items of similar nature. Resident rates are subject to regular review.
