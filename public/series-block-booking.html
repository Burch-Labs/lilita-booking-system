📘 Series Block Booking SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

Step 1: Collect Client Details

  • Get client full names & contact info
  • Confirm group size & number of rooms
  • Identify preferred package type
  • Note any special requests

Step 2: Select Dates & Rates

  • Check calendar for availability
  • Identify peak/shoulder/low season dates
  • Calculate per-night rates accordingly
  • Confirm total with customer

Step 3: Create Block Reservation

  • Enter details in series block form
  • Review auto-calculated pricing
  • Verify dates on calendar
  • Submit block (48-hour hold)

Step 4: Payment & Confirmation

  • Share proposal with 30% deposit link
  • Confirm balance due 45 days before
  • Issue booking confirmation code
  • Send WhatsApp updates
⏰ Important Deadlines:
• 48-hour block hold (then auto-releases if not confirmed)
• 30% deposit required to confirm booking
• Balance due 45 days before arrival
• Peak season (Jun-Aug) books 3-6 months in advance
